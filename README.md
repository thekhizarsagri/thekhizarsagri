**AI-powered Business Intelligence & Analytics Platform**

A full-stack platform designed to help businesses understand their production, sales, inventory and financial performance.

### Features

- 📦 Inventory Management
- 💰 Sales & Expense Tracking
- 📈 Business Analytics
- 🔮 Demand Forecasting
- 📊 Product Performance Analysis
- 🎯 Break-even Analysis
- 📦 EOQ & Reorder Point
- 📉 Dead Stock Detection
- 💡 AI-powered Business Insights
- 📊 KPI Health Score
